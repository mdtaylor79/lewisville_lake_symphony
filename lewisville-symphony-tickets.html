Frequently Asked Questions

Ticket & Visit Questions

Can I buy tickets at the door?
Yes, tickets are available at the venue on the night of each concert, subject to availability. We recommend purchasing in advance online to guarantee your seats.
Is there a discount for students?
Yes — student tickets are $10 per individual concert and $35 for the full season subscription. A valid student ID is required at the door.
How do military discounts work?
Active duty military and their family members attend for $10 per person. Call 972.874.9087 or email us at question@lewisvillesymphony.org to receive your promo code for online purchase.
What are the benefits of a season subscription?
Season subscribers receive priority seating, rehearsal observation privileges (on request), and discount tickets for friends and family — plus peace of mind knowing your seats are locked in all season.
Do I need a ticket for the Chamber Series?
No — all International Chamber Series concerts at Trinity Presbyterian Church are completely free and open to the public. No ticket, no RSVP required.
Is the venue wheelchair accessible?
Yes. Accessible seating is available at the Lewisville Grand Theater. Please contact the box office in advance at 972.874.9087 so we can best accommodate you.
Can I get a refund or exchange my ticket?
Please contact our box office directly to discuss exchanges or refund options. We'll do our best to find a solution that works for you.
How do group tickets work?
Groups of any size are welcome — churches, bus tours, social clubs, and families. Contact us directly for our best group rates and to arrange concierge assistance.
