Symphony Interns

A Front-Row Seat to Professional Music-Making

The Lewisville Lake Symphony selects its Symphony Interns from students in the 8th through 12th grades who are excited by great music and committed to their community.

"We were looking for a select number of students who are excited by great music. In return for helping the House Front team at performances, the Interns get community service credits and an opportunity during final rehearsals to sit on stage among members of the Lewisville Lake Symphony all-professional orchestra under the baton of maestro Adron Ming."

— Margaret Wells, Chair, LLS Civic Relationships Group

Program Mission: To foster the long-term enjoyment of great music and to encourage student musicians.

  • Assist the House Front team at each Symphony Series concert
  • Earn community service credit hours
  • Sit on stage among the professional orchestra at final rehearsals
  • Gain behind-the-scenes experience with a professional ensemble
  • Build a foundation for the Associates program

Symphony Associates

Continue Your Journey with the Symphony

Following the two-semester Intern term, students are invited to become Symphony Associates and continue contributing to the organization by providing assistance at concerts.

This extended involvement allows young musicians and arts enthusiasts to deepen their connection to the LLS through high school and into their college years.

  • Continue through college graduation
  • Provide concert assistance each season

Scout Merit Badge Program

Earn Your Music Merit Badge

The Lewisville Lake Symphony is proud to offer a free program helping Scouts of America and Girl Scouts fulfill the requirements for music-related merit badges through live orchestral engagement.

This is a unique opportunity for scouts to connect with professional musicians, experience a world-class orchestra up close, and fulfill badge requirements in a memorable way.
