Youth & Education

Inspiring the Next Generation of Musicians

The Lewisville Lake Symphony is deeply committed to music education and youth enrichment throughout our community. From internships to merit badges, we offer pathways for young people to engage with live classical music.

Internship Program

Fall 2026 internship positions open through August 31. Gain hands-on experience with a professional orchestra.

Scout Merit Badge Program

Free program helping Scouts of America and Girl Scouts earn music-related merit badges through live orchestral experience.

Vernell Gregg Young Artists Competition

Annual competition for emerging soloists, with the winner performing with the full orchestra in April 2027.

Annual Competition

Vernell Gregg Young Artists Competition 2027

One of the region's most prestigious competitions for young classical musicians. The winner performs as soloist with the Lewisville Lake Symphony at our April 2027 concert — a career-defining opportunity.

Deadline: December 30, 2026
