Youth · Annual

Vernell Gregg Young Artists Competition

One of North Texas's premier youth music competitions, the Vernell Gregg Young Artists Competition gives students in grades 6 through 12 the opportunity to perform before a professional panel and compete for a $1,000 Grand Prize — plus the unforgettable experience of performing with a live professional orchestra at the LLS April concert.

Prizes & Recognition

  • All finalists receive recognition in concert programs and the opportunity to perform before a live audience.

International · Two-Year Cycle

LLS International Competition

The LLS International Competition alternates each year between Piano and Voice, offering pre-professional musicians a $2,000 first prize and the opportunity to perform as soloist with a full professional orchestra. The competition is open nationally and internationally to college and university students pursuing careers in performance.
