Our Mission

Enriching North Texas Through Live Music

"The Lewisville Lake Symphony is dedicated to enhancing the quality of life for people of all ages in North Texas through live and inspiring classical music. The symphony is committed to educating young audiences and supporting rising talent."

Since 1984, the Lewisville Lake Symphony has been dedicated to bringing exceptional music and meaningful programs to North Texas. Each season features four full symphony concerts and four chamber performances, as well as family favorites like Peter & the Wolf.

Our educational outreach serves thousands of students, including a special concert for every third grader in LISD, internships for middle school through university students, and competitions that award scholarships and performance opportunities. We also offer a Scout merit badge program and invite underserved children and adults to experience live music at no cost. More than concerts, we're building a vibrant, inclusive community through music.

Music Director & Conductor
Adron Ming
"Adron Ming, Music Director and Conductor of the Lewisville Lake Symphony has presided over the orchestra since its inception, now one of the most respected regional orchestras in Texas." — Dallas Morning News

Music Director/Conductor Adron Ming has led the orchestra since its inception. A native of Weslaco, Texas, Maestro Ming received his master's degree in music theory from Baylor University, where he studied cello and chamber music with Lev Aronson and conducting with Daniel Sternberg. He also served on the Baylor faculty as instructor of music theory and assistant conductor of the Baylor and Waco Symphonies.

While attending the University of North Texas, Ming studied conducting with Anshel Brusilow before joining the faculty of Bethel College in St. Paul, Minnesota, as assistant professor of music and conductor of the Bethel Chamber Symphony.

Ming has conducted the Toledo Symphony Orchestra, the Taipei Sunshine Symphony Orchestra in Taiwan, the Plano Symphony, Irving Symphony, the Chamber Symphony of the Metrocrest, and the New Philharmonic of Irving. He served as associate conductor of the Richardson Symphony until 2010, and in 2016 became Director of the String Orchestra at the University of Texas at Dallas.

Assistant Conductor
Nathan Howard

Nathan Howard is a multi-instrumentalist and conductor pursuing a Doctorate at the University of North Texas under Maestro David Itkin. Following graduate studies at Southern Methodist University, he served as resident conductor of the Meadows Symphony Orchestra.

Nathan also studied at the Indiana University Jacobs School of Music, where he recorded with the New Music Ensemble. As an ensemble leader, he served as Music Director for The Camp-of-the-Woods jazz band and has conducted the Lviv National Philharmonic, the Meadows Symphony Orchestra, and the Samford University Orchestra.

His conducting teachers include Paul C. Phillips, Jack Delaney, and Michelle Merrill.

Executive Director
Nancy Wright

Nancy Wright became Lewisville Lake Symphony's first Executive Director in 2024 after many years of deep involvement with the orchestra — first as a flutist performing with the ensemble and later as an active volunteer in many organizational roles.

After earning bachelor's and master's degrees in flute performance from the University of Houston and West Texas A&M, studying with Byron Hester and Gary Garner, Nancy was active in the music world before transitioning to a career in business with IBM. There she worked until retirement across roles spanning technical support, project management, and people management.

The combination of her music background and business expertise has enabled her to expand the breadth of the Symphony's program offerings and build an organizational structure for its all-volunteer workforce.

Equity & Diversity

Music for Everyone

"The Lewisville Lake Symphony is committed to the principles of equity, racial equality, and diversity when hiring orchestral musicians and guest artists and in the audiences we serve."

We are committed to including all individuals regardless of race, nationality, gender, age, sexual orientation, or sexual identification both as performers and audience members. We believe that fully utilizing the talents of all individuals allows us to use our concert and outreach programs to foster and strengthen artistic connections within the greater DFW community and beyond.
